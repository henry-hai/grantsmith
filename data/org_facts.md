## Mission

ArtHouse Studio promotes equity, mental wellness, and community connection
through youth creativity. The organization works to help level the playing
field through the power of creativity and innovation, incorporating
social-emotional and mental health tools into its curriculum.

## Who we serve

- Youth in under-resourced school communities throughout the Bay Area
- A focus on engaging with Title I schools and connecting them with
  corporations and community
- Serving underserved student populations regardless of economic background
  or school resources
- Number of students served each year: TO FILL IN

## Programs

- **Art In The Schools.** Day programs, after-school sessions, and on-demand
  classroom videos that bring arts education directly into schools.
- **Art Programs For Wellness.** Creative tools for school wellness centers,
  professional workshops, and classroom management support that treat
  creativity as a path to mental wellness.
- **Youth Exhibits / Corporate Impact.** Community and corporate art
  installations designed for connection, understanding, and inspiration,
  giving student work a public platform.
- **Creativity for Life Programs.** Programming designed to develop student
  leadership and innovative thinking.

## Approach

Teaching artists empower students to develop as innovative thinkers,
communicators, and change-makers. Programs aim to deeply engage youth, build
their confidence, and develop innovative and inventive mindsets. The
organization wants every student to see that their creativity can take them
far, no matter what direction they choose.
