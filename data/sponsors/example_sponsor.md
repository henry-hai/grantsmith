# Sponsor Context Template

> Copy this file per sponsor, for example data/sponsors/adobe.md, and fill it
> in. Pass it with --sponsor-file. You can also pass one or more --sponsor-url
> pages from the sponsor's own site, and GrantSmith will combine both. Include
> only things you can stand behind. GrantSmith will not invent sponsor facts,
> and neither should this file.

## Sponsor name

Example: Adobe

## What they fund

Their stated giving priorities and focus areas. For example youth creativity,
digital skills, arts access, education equity.

## Why we are a fit

The honest points of alignment between the sponsor's priorities and ArtHouse
Studio's mission and programs.

## Relationship and history

Any prior contact, past gifts, current program officer, warm introductions, or
existing partnership. Leave blank if this is a cold ask.

## The specific ask

The program, amount, and time frame this proposal is requesting.

## Notes for the writer

Anything else Julie wants reflected in the tone or emphasis of the answers.
