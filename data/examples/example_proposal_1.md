## Who we are

ArtHouse Studio is a San Jose based 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2020,
born out of ArtHouse Kids and built on a simple conviction: creativity can
level the playing field. We work with under-resourced school communities
across the Bay Area, with a particular focus on Title I schools, to promote
equity, mental wellness, and community connection through youth creativity.

## The need

In too many Bay Area schools, arts education is the first thing cut and the
last thing restored. The students who lose it are the ones with the fewest
alternatives. We built Art In The Schools because we believe a student's
school budget should never decide whether they get to make art, and because
we have watched what happens when a classroom gets real creative instruction:
students who were quiet start communicating, and students who were counted
out start seeing themselves as innovative thinkers and change-makers.

## What your support makes possible

A grant of [AMOUNT] would support a full year of Art In The Schools
programming at [NUMBER] partner school sites. That includes day programs,
after-school sessions, and our on-demand classroom videos, which extend the
reach of our teaching artists into classrooms we cannot visit in person.
Every element of the curriculum incorporates the social-emotional and mental
health tools that are central to how we teach.

## What success looks like

We measure engagement, confidence, and creative growth, and we put student
work in front of the community. When ArtHouse youth helped create a
100-foot-high video exhibit in downtown San Jose, thousands of people saw
what young artists from under-resourced schools can do when someone hands
them the tools. That is the outcome we are asking you to invest in.
