## Organization background

ArtHouse Studio promotes equity, mental wellness, and community connection
through youth creativity. Founded in 2020 as an independent nonprofit growing
out of ArtHouse Kids, we serve youth in under-resourced school communities
throughout the Bay Area. Our teaching artists do more than teach technique.
They empower students to grow as innovative thinkers, communicators, and
change-makers, and they weave social-emotional and mental health tools into
every lesson.

## The project: Art Programs For Wellness

Schools are asking for help with student mental health, and we believe art
belongs in that answer. Art Programs For Wellness brings creative tools into
school wellness centers, offers professional workshops for educators, and
supports classroom management through creativity. For a student sitting in a
wellness center on a hard day, a guided creative practice can be the bridge
back to feeling capable and connected.

## Why it matters

We want every student to know that their creativity can take them far, no
matter what direction they choose. When a school wellness center becomes a

starts being something they have felt firsthand. Your partnership would let
us bring that experience to more of the students who need it most.
