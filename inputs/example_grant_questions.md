# Bright Futures Foundation: Youth Program Grant Application

Please answer each question in 500 words or fewer.

1. Describe your organization's mission and history. What need does your organization address, and who do you serve?
2. Describe the program or project for which you are requesting funding. What are its goals and key activities?
3. How does your organization involve the community it serves in the design and delivery of its programs?
4. What outcomes do you expect this program to achieve, and how will you measure success?
5. Describe your organization's capacity to carry out this work, including staff, leadership, and relevant experience.
6. How will this program be sustained beyond the grant period?
